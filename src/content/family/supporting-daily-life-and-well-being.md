### Whānau can help by:

* Assisting with appointments, transport, or medication reminders.

* Encouraging rest, movement, and balanced nutrition.

* Helping maintain normal life rhythms — hobbies, social connection, and small joys.

* Being patient with changes in mood, sleep, or physical comfort.
