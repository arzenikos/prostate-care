## What’s a Gleason score?

It’s a number doctors use to show how aggressive prostate cancer is. Lower scores mean slower-growing cancer; higher scores mean it may spread faster.

### What Stage 1 means, in plain terms

Stage 1 usually means the cancer is still inside the prostate and was found early. That gives you more time and more choices.

### First-line treatment options

Depending on your Gleason score, PSA, and overall health, your team may talk about active surveillance, surgery, or radiation. The “right” path is the one that fits your cancer and your life.

### What staying on active surveillance looks like

Active surveillance is not “doing nothing.” It is a planned schedule of PSA tests, reviews, and sometimes scans or biopsies, so treatment can start if the cancer changes.
