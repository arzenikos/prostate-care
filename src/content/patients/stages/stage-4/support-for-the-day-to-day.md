Food choices during treatment can change from day to day. Eat what feels manageable, keep fluids nearby, and talk with your care team if appetite or weight changes become a concern.

## Eating what feels right

There is no single “cancer diet” that fits everyone. Familiar food, smaller portions, and flavours you still enjoy are a good starting point.

If cooking is too much, frozen meals, soup, or help from whānau still count as care.

## Sleep, pain relief, and symptom tools

Tell your team about pain, nausea, or nights that never settle. There are usually options to try — medication, positioning, or quiet routines.

## Movement, touch, or stillness

A stretch, a hand held, or simply sitting in the sun can be enough. What works is whatever helps you feel present in your body today.
