## Treatment versus comfort decisions

The “right” plan is the one that matches your goals. That may still include treatment, or it may centre on comfort. You can revisit the choice.

## What palliative support actually means

Palliative care is extra support for symptoms, sleep, pain, and the emotional load. It is not the same as giving up.

## Being heard by your team

Say what matters most in the first minutes of a visit. Bring a support person if it helps the conversation stay on track.
