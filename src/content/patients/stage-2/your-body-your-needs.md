## Managing side effects through food and rest

Treatment can change appetite, sleep, and energy. Eat what sits well, keep fluids nearby, and rest when your body asks.

## Handling fatigue and disrupted sleep

Fatigue is common. Short rests, a regular wind-down, and telling your care team about sudden changes are all part of looking after yourself.

## Keeping active within your limits

Movement can be a walk to the letterbox or a stretch in a chair. The aim is kindness to your body, not a personal best.
