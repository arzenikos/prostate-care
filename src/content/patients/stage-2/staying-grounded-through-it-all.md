## Coping with “what if” thoughts

A new or changing diagnosis can fill the day with “what if.” Naming the worry — on paper, with a friend, or with a clinician — often takes some of its heat away.

This page is still being built out. Until then, these starting points can help:

- Know when to ask for help, and who to turn to (your GP, specialist nurse, or a support group).
- Use short check-ins: sleep, mood, appetite, and energy.
- Keep one conversation going with someone who will listen without trying to fix everything.

If you or someone you love is in crisis, contact local emergency services or a mental health helpline in your area.
