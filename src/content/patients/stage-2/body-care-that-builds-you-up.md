## Kegel exercise

Kegel exercises are a simple, no-equipment way to boost bladder control and support prostate recovery — just a few squeezes a day can make a big difference.

<p class="media-placeholder">Placeholder: kegel exercise steps</p>

1. Identify the muscles: try stopping urination midstream — that’s your pelvic floor.
2. Contract: squeeze and hold for 3–5 seconds, then release.
3. Repeat: do 10 reps, 2–3 times a day.
4. Consistency matters: do them daily for best results.
