## Gentle nutrition and hydration

Small meals, familiar flavours, and sips of water or soup can be easier than three large plates. If eating becomes hard, tell your team.

## Fatigue, sleep disruption, and what helps

Tiredness can be physical and emotional. Protect rest, and let people help with the practical load.

## Movement when you can — or resting when you need

There is no prize for pushing through. A short stretch counts. So does a day in bed if that is what recovery looks like today.
