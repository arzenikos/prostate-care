## Naming difficult emotions

Fear, anger, and numbness can all show up. They are responses, not failures.

## Support groups that feel real

Look for groups where you can speak plainly — in person or online. Whānau can come too if that helps.

## Keeping some control in the chaos

Choose one small thing you still decide: a walk, a playlist, who sits in the waiting room. Control does not have to be large to be real.
