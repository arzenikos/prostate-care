## The Stage 3 treatment landscape

Stage 3 usually means the cancer has grown beyond the prostate but has not spread to distant organs. Treatment often involves more than one team.

## Coordinating care across teams

You may see urology, radiation oncology, medical oncology, and nursing. Ask who the main contact is so questions have a clear home.

## Understanding more complex symptoms

New pain, urinary changes, or swelling should be reported promptly. Write down when symptoms start so visits stay focused.
